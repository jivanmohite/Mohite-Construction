Construction guide

Key details for a concrete road project

Prepare these details before discussing concrete road construction or repair.

1. Road dimensions

Note the approximate length, width and any changes in road level. A site sketch or photographs can help explain the requirement.

2. Intended use

Share whether the road is for pedestrian use, two-wheelers, cars or heavier vehicles. Design specifications should be determined by qualified professionals.

3. Drainage and ground conditions

Water flow, existing surface condition and subgrade preparation are important considerations for durable road work.

4. Existing road repair

For repair, explain where cracks, settlement or damaged sections are visible and whether water collects at the site.

5. Confirm the scope

Discuss preparation, materials, joints, curing, access restrictions and the expected work schedule.

This article is general planning information, not engineering or legal advice. Consult qualified professionals for drawings, structural design, permissions and technical specifications.
