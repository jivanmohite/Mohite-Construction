Understanding stone masonry work

Key points to discuss before beginning stone construction.

Choose the purpose of the wall or structure

Tell the contractor whether the work is a boundary wall, retaining structure or another masonry application. Structural requirements differ.

Discuss stone and workmanship

Stone type, size, availability, mortar specification and finish influence the work. Confirm the material and finish expectations before starting.

Consider site conditions

Access for material delivery, ground conditions, levels and drainage can affect planning.

Use the right technical guidance

For retaining or load-bearing applications, obtain a design from a qualified structural professional. Do not rely on appearance alone to determine safety.

This article is general planning information, not engineering or legal advice. Consult qualified professionals for drawings, structural design, permissions and technical specifications.
